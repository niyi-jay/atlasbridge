Who we are

A Houston small business built for federal work.

AtlasBridge Group LLC delivers building trade, facility support and administrative services to federal agencies, as a prime contractor and as a subcontractor to other primes. We are registered and active in the System for Award Management, and we compete on Total Small Business set-asides. We quote fixed price, we price from the wage determination in the solicitation, and we submit complete packages the first time.

How we work

What you get from us on a solicitation.

A responsive package, not a price in an email

Every required form completed and signed, amendments acknowledged, and certifications answered as written. We read the whole solicitation, including the parts that are easy to miss, and we ask the contracting officer before the question deadline rather than guess.

Labour priced from the wage determination

Where the Service Contract Act or Davis-Bacon applies, we price against the determination attached to the solicitation and flow the clauses down to our subcontractors, with certified payroll from day one.

Subcontractors we have checked

We qualify trade partners on scope, schedule, wage compliance and insurance before we put a number in front of the Government, so the price we quote is the price we perform at.

Work that respects an occupied building

Most of what we do happens in facilities that stay in use. We phase the work, protect the space, clear debris daily, and coordinate access and outages with the facility manager.
